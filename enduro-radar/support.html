Enduro Radar Support

Enduro Radar is an app for enduro riders: a live map of your crew, hills with fresh conditions, chats with voice messages and SOS to your whole city. A closed community — invitation only.

Contact

  • E-mail: Robert.stanislavovich@gmail.com — questions, bugs, account deletion, reports.
  • In the app: "Me" → "Support" — an idea, a bug or anything else; the reply arrives there.
  • Reports about users and content are reviewed within 24 hours.

Developer and data controller — Robert Sasimovich (Сасимович Роберт Станиславович), an individual, Republic of Belarus.

How to join

Only with a personal invitation code from a member. Sign in with Telegram, and on iPhone also with Apple. After signing in: city, accepting the rules and the privacy policy, profile.

Report and block

  • Report a message, photo or video, user or spot: long-press a message in a chat, or open a person's profile, a photo or a spot → "Report". Reports go to the admins immediately.
  • Block from a person's profile or a direct chat: neither of you sees the other's position, you can't message each other, and you get no notifications from them. The list and "Unblock" — "Me" → "Privacy".

Location and "Riding"

Your position is sent only in "Riding" mode, which you turn on yourself, and only members of your city see it. The mode turns itself off after an hour without movement and after 8 hours in any case; within 100 m of a hill others see only "at the hill", without a point. Only you see your ride tracks. Details — in the privacy policy.

SOS

Hold SOS for 3 seconds and pick a reason — members of your city get an alert. The app does not replace emergency services: if you are injured, call 112.

Delete your account

"Me" → "Delete account" — immediately and permanently, with your rides, direct conversations and support messages. No access to the app — write to Robert.stanislavovich@gmail.com and we will delete it manually within 30 days.
