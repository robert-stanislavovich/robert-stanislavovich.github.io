Enduro Radar Privacy Policy

Version of 25 November 2026. Applies to the Enduro Radar app for iPhone and Android and its server.

1. Who is responsible

Data controller and developer of the app: Robert Sasimovich (Сасимович Роберт Станиславович), an individual, Republic of Belarus. Questions, access, correction and deletion requests: Robert.stanislavovich@gmail.com.

Enduro Radar is a closed community of enduro riders: you can join with a personal invitation from another member or through an access request approved by an administrator. The app is free and has no ads. We do not sell data or share it with ad networks. There are no third-party analytics or trackers in the app.

Access request. If you have no invite code, after signing in with Telegram or Apple you can request access: take a photo with the in-app camera of your bike (or yourself in riding gear) next to a sheet with the check code the app gives you, and answer a few questions. This lets an administrator make sure you really ride enduro — the community is closed. From the Telegram ID the administrator sees a rough estimate of when the Telegram account was created (IDs are issued in order; this is an estimate, not an exact date). Only Enduro Radar administrators see the request (in the admin panel and in the notification to the administrator via the Telegram bot); other members never see it. Photos, answers and sign-in data from the request are kept until the decision and 30 more days, then deleted; only the sign-in account ID, the decision, its reason and date remain — so that after a refusal a new request can be sent no sooner than 30 days later. If you delete an account created from a request, the request is deleted at once. The request contains no location; the photo is compressed on the phone without the place where it was taken.

Legal bases: your consent at sign-up and when enabling features (location, notifications); performance of the contract — providing the service you ask for; legitimate interest — community safety and moderation.

3. Location

  • Your position is sent only in "Riding" mode, which you switch on yourself. After an hour without movement the app asks whether you are still riding (with no answer the mode stays on); after 8 hours it asks again; the mode is switched off with “Stop”. On Android a persistent notification is shown the whole time.
  • While the mode is on, the phone sends its position at the rate you choose — every 10 minutes, 5 minutes, minute (the default) or 15 seconds (the slider on the "Riding" bar; on a low battery the rate drops automatically) — also in the background and with the screen off, otherwise friends can't find you in the woods. Without network, points are queued on the phone and sent together; the server keeps only the latest one.
  • The live map keeps no history. For the map the server keeps one latest point (rounded to ~10 m), overwritten by the next one. After 30 minutes without a new point you disappear from the map; when you stop riding — immediately.
  • Ride tracks are for you only. While "Riding" is on, the phone records your path (a point about every 5 seconds or every 10 metres) to show it to you on the map and, after you stop, a ride summary. The track is stored on the phone and on the server (in the EU) so it isn't lost when you change phones. Neither other members nor admins can see your track: the server gives it only to you. Delete one ride or all of them in "Me" → "My rides" — they are deleted on the phone and on the server. Deleting your account erases all tracks.
  • Sharing a ride is something only you do: the app makes a picture (map with the track, places and the numbers) and hands it to the system "Share" menu. The picture shows the whole track, including the start and the finish — keep that in mind if the ride starts at home. You choose where to send it; from then on it is subject to that app's rules.
  • Routes. From a saved ride you can make a route — a line without time or speed — and publish it so that others can ride it. Publishing is reviewed by an admin. A published route is visible to all members of the app; via the route link anyone, even without the app, sees the picture, the name, the distance and the elevation gain, but not the line itself and no coordinates. Before publishing you set points A and B yourself: nothing outside them (for example the road from your home) is shown to anyone. You can share a route by link without publishing it — whoever has the link can see it (you can reset the link, and the old one stops working). You can unpublish or delete a published route at any time. You can add points to a route (a ford, a hazard, a gate, etc.) with a name, a description and photos — they are published together with the route after an admin's review; photos are stored without coordinates and deleted with the point or the route. "Navigate" opens an external navigator and passes it only the coordinates of point A or B and the route name.
  • Route completions. In "Riding" mode the phone itself notices that you are riding along a published route and, after you stop, saves the time and speed of completing published routes (start and end on the line, average and top speed, direction) — without coordinates. Only you see your completions; others see only how many people have ridden the route and the best time. A completion can be deleted; deleting the route or your account removes them.
  • Profile. Other members see in your profile the spots where you rode (the spot and the day of the last visit, no coordinates or times), your published routes and — unless you switch it off — your private routes (name, distance, region; they can be opened and saved). Both can be switched off in "Me" → "Privacy".
  • 100 m rule. Within 100 m of a hill (the radius can be larger for a big hill), others only see "at hill X" in the hill counter, not a point on the map. The server removes the coordinates; they never reach other phones. Away from hills your position is shown as a point.
  • "Show only the spot, not my dot" ("Me" → "Privacy"). When on, others see you only as a number in the counter of the spot you are at, and away from spots they don't see you at all; the server gives your point on the map to no one, admins included. The setting is stored on the server and applies on any of your devices. It does not affect SOS: an SOS always goes out with the exact point.
  • "Riding" reminders ("Me" → "Notifications") only remind you to turn the mode on: it starts only when you tap "Let's go". "On a schedule" and "Match my habits" are worked out by the phone itself — from your settings and the start times of your rides — and the phone schedules the notification itself, sending nothing. If you turn on spot reminders ("Always" location access is needed), your phone notices you're at a spot by itself and sends nothing: the zones around the nearest hills and meeting points are watched by the phone's system, and the "you're at a spot" event stays on the phone. "Friends are riding" is a push from the server when two people you know are riding in your city at the same time: people you have messaged with (both of you wrote) or who were at the same hill at the same time in the last 60 days (from your ride summaries — hills and times, not tracks). The push contains names and the spot name, no coordinates; the server only remembers the time of the last such reminder. Each reminder can be turned off in the same place.
  • Only members who chose the same city (for "Other" — within 100 km) see your position. If you blocked someone or they blocked you, neither sees the other's position.
  • For statistics the server remembers, for the current day only, the fact "rode in this city today" — no points, no times; the list is erased every midnight (Minsk time).
  • SOS is always sent with a location: members of your city (except blocked ones) and admins see it while the SOS is active; while active, the point updates every 15 seconds. The location of a closed SOS is kept for 30 days after it is closed, to review incidents, and then erased; the SOS itself (reason, time, who responded) is kept until the account is deleted.
  • The one-off "More precise with GPS" fix for choosing a city is not stored; only the chosen city is (for "Other" — a point with ~1 km precision).

4. Who sees your data

  • Other members: name, picture, motorcycle, position in "Riding" mode under the rules above, your messages and photos in hill chats, condition marks, SOS. Direct messages are visible only to you and the other person.
  • Community admins: what moderation needs: profiles, reports and the reported content, spot suggestions, active SOS. Direct messages are not available to admins, except a single message reported by a participant of that conversation — admins see it in the report queue.
  • Messages from Enduro Radar. We may write to you in the app on behalf of Enduro Radar (news, important safety information); your replies are read by an administrator. This conversation appears in "Messages" with an "official" mark; you don't have to read it, and you can turn off news notifications ("Me" → "Notifications" → "Enduro Radar news"). To choose who gets a message (for example, only Android or only a newer version), the server knows your platform (iOS/Android), where the app was installed from (App Store, Google Play or APK) and the app version. When you reply, the administrator gets a notification through the Telegram bot (name and the start of the text). The conversation is deleted together with your account.
  • Processors (only to run the service):
  • Cloudflare, Inc. — hosting of the server, database and files (Workers, D1, Durable Objects, R2). Data is placed in the European Union ("Eastern Europe" region).
  • Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and Apple (APNs) — push delivery: they receive the device token and the notification text (e.g. the sender's name and the beginning of a message).
  • Telegram, Apple — sign-in. The Telegram bot receives your Telegram ID and the /start message with a one-time code; the server downloads your Telegram profile photo once, at sign-up. With Apple sign-in the server receives an access token from Apple and stores it encrypted — only to revoke the app's access to your Apple ID when you delete your account.
  • Telegram — notifications to the administrator. About new SOS (name, reason, nearest hill — no coordinates), reports, spot suggestions, messages to "Support" (name, city, the first 300 characters of the text, phone model and versions) and access requests (the first photo, name and Telegram username, answers, check code) the server notifies the administrator through the Telegram bot. If you signed in with Telegram, the same bot tells you the decision on your request.
  • OpenFreeMap — base map: the phone downloads map tiles, so the tile server sees the IP address and which map area was requested. It does not receive your "Riding" position.
  • Esri (ArcGIS) — the "Satellite" layer, only if you turn it on: the phone downloads imagery tiles, so Esri's server sees the IP address and which area was requested. It does not receive your "Riding" position.
  • Google Play and App Store — optional tips to the developer (“Me” → “Support Enduro Radar”): the store processes the payment under its own terms. We receive no payment details, name or e-mail of the buyer; our server does not know about the purchase — the “thank you” counter is kept only on your phone. Nothing is unlocked in return.
  • We do not share data with third parties for advertising and do not sell it. Disclosure required by law — only upon an official request of an authorised body, in cases provided for by the laws of the Republic of Belarus.

6. Deleting your account

Delete your account in the app: "Me" → "Delete account". Immediately and irreversibly: - sign-in methods, all sessions, push tokens, picture, motorcycle, city and "Other" point, your SOS with locations and responses, blocks, room memberships and invitation requests are deleted; unused invitations are revoked; - if you signed in with Apple, the app's access to your Apple ID is revoked with Apple and the stored token is deleted; - your position is removed from the map and "Riding" mode is switched off; - route completions and the spot visit history are deleted — completion times and speeds, the spots and days of visits shown in the profile; - all rides with their tracks are deleted — summaries and the tracks themselves; route drafts and routes under review too; - published routes stay in the app (without your name — as from "Deleted user"): you gave them to the community; if you want them gone, unpublish or delete them before deleting the account; - direct conversations are deleted entirely, including photos, videos and voice messages — for both participants; - messages to "Support" are deleted entirely; - messages, photos and marks in shared hill chats stay but are anonymised: shown as from "Deleted user", without a name or picture; - signing in again with the same Telegram or Apple account requires a new invitation.

If you can't use the app, write to Robert.stanislavovich@gmail.com and tell us the Telegram account or Apple ID you signed in with (or your profile name) — we will delete the account manually within 30 days.

7. Your rights

You can find out what data we have about you, correct it (name, picture, motorcycle, city — in "Me"), withdraw consent (switch off "Riding", location and notifications in the phone settings), and delete your account. Requests: Robert.stanislavovich@gmail.com. You may complain to the personal data protection authority — in the Republic of Belarus, the National Personal Data Protection Center.

8. Children

The app is intended for people aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect children's data.

9. Security

HTTPS only. No passwords: sign-in via Telegram or Apple. Session tokens are stored on the server as hashes. Media is served only with a valid session. Server logs contain no coordinates or tokens (checked by an automated test).

10. Changes

We will announce material changes in the app. Current version: https://robert-stanislavovich.github.io/enduro-radar/privacy.html.
