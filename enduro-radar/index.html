Enduro Radar is an app for enduro riders who want to ride together. Open it and you instantly see a map with hills and key places — and everyone who is riding right now.

Invitation only

This is a closed community: you can join only with a personal code from a member. Good hills don't turn into a public map, and sharing your position feels safe — only your crew sees it.

Live map

  • Tap "Riding" and your crew sees you on the city map. Your position updates once a minute to save battery.
  • Under every hill — how many people are riding there right now.
  • On a hill you're shown only in the "at the hill" counter, without a point. Away from hills — as a point, so you're easy to find.
  • Riding mode switches off by itself after an hour without movement.
  • See your own path on the map and, after "Stop", a ride summary: distance, time, speed, elevation gain, hills. Only you see your tracks and you delete them yourself; share a picture with the track and hills if you like.
  • Turn a ride into a route and share it as a link — others can ride it. You decide which part to show (points A and B).

Hills and places

  • Hills, meeting points, repair shops, fuel stations, shops, fords, dangerous spots, places where rangers or police are on duty.
  • Every place has a description, difficulty, directions, parking, a rating and a photo and video gallery.
  • Fresh condition marks: "dry", "mud", "closed", "forester" — with author and time.
  • Suggest a new place or an edit — an admin reviews and publishes it.
  • Get directions in your favourite navigation app.

Chats

  • Every hill has its own room: text, photos, videos up to 60 seconds and voice messages up to 2 minutes.
  • Replies and mentions, follow your favourite hills.
  • Direct messages to any member.

SOS

  • Crashed, broke down, got stuck — hold SOS for 3 seconds and pick a reason.
  • Everyone in your city gets an urgent notification, the nearest first, with the distance.
  • See who responded "On my way". Without network, SOS is sent as soon as you're back online.
  • The app does not replace emergency services: if you're injured, call 112.

Cities

Minsk, Brest, Vitebsk, Gomel, Grodno, Mogilev — each together with its district. Not near any of them? "Other" shows everything within 100 km around you.

Privacy

  • Only your crew sees your position, and only in Riding mode. Ride tracks — only you.
  • Blocked users don't see each other.
  • Reports on messages, photos or people go to the admin.
  • Delete your account in the app with one button.

Sign in with Telegram, and on iPhone also with Apple. Russian and English, light and dark theme.
