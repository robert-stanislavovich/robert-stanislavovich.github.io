Enduro Radar Community Rules

Enduro Radar is a closed community of enduro riders. It shows who is riding where and lets you call for help. It only works as long as we trust each other.

1. Friends vouch for friends

Invite people you'd ride with. By inviting someone, you vouch for them: if they seriously break the rules, the admin may ask you about it too. Don't sell invitation codes or post them in public channels.

2. SOS only when it matters

SOS alerts the whole city and breaks through "Do Not Disturb". Use it when you or someone next to you really needs help. If you no longer need help, tap "No help needed" right away. A fake SOS as a joke means a block. The app does not replace emergency services: if someone is injured, call 112.

3. Respect the spots

Don't ride where it's forbidden: nature reserves, private land, crop fields, beaches. Don't leave litter, don't make noise near homes, don't annoy locals or rangers. Don't publish spots that the owner or a club asked to keep quiet.

4. Honest marks and suggestions

Mark a hill's condition ("dry", "mud", "closed", "forester") only if you've been there yourself, recently. When suggesting a new spot or an edit, write what you know for sure.

5. No abuse

Not allowed: insults, harassment, threats, hate speech on any grounds, spam and advertising, fraud, impersonation.

6. Respect privacy

Don't post photos of people or licence plates without their consent. Don't publish other people's personal data (phone, address) or a person's location they haven't shared themselves. Don't forward other people's direct messages.

7. Prohibited content

Zero tolerance: pornography and sexual content, violence and gore, calls for dangerous or illegal actions, selling prohibited goods, copyright infringement. Such content is removed and the author is blocked.

8. Safety on the trail

Ride with protection and within your limits. Don't film while riding. "Riding" mode shows you to your crew — switch it off when you don't want to be seen.

How we enforce the rules

  • Report a message, photo or video, user or spot with the "Report" button. Reports go to the admins immediately.
  • Block a person from their profile or a chat: neither of you sees the other's position, you can't send each other direct messages, and you get no notifications from them (messages in shared hill chats stay visible).
  • Admins review reports within 24 hours: they delete messages, hide photos and spots, and block the offender's account. A block takes effect immediately: the person is signed out on all devices and disappears from the map.
  • To discuss an admin decision: Robert.stanislavovich@gmail.com. Write there too about objectionable content if you can't report it in the app.
